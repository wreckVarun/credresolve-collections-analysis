The ₹10 Cr decision

Hold the ₹10 Cr for one quarter.

The six options differ by less than our measurement error. Three of them are channel bets, and channel effect cannot be measured here: 36% of recovery arrives untouched, and another 18% on two or more channels at once.

Agent investment cannot be sized either — agent identity is unrecoverable in the source data, and recovery per agent-hour has been flat at ₹16.1–17.0 K for seven months regardless of staffing.

There is no cost table in this data. Any ROI, break-even or payback figure would be invented.

Spend ₹40–60 lakh instead Randomised targeting holdout (8 weeks, ~1,700 control account-months — enough to detect a 10% lift at 80% power), plus identity resolution and per-channel cost instrumentation.
What it buys If the experiment finds a lever with a 5% lift, that is ₹9–11 Cr of incremental annual recovery. Cost of waiting: ~₹2.5 Cr of deferred opportunity. Cost of not waiting: ₹10 Cr against a signal we have shown to be a calendar artefact.

Why parts of this screen are blank

Four defects invalidate an entire class of analysis. These are not caveats — they are the reason four questions have no answer above.

SEV 1 Borrower identity is inconsistent. 30,600 rows resolve to 11,015 IDs; 8,468 carry conflicting names, phones and cities. One ID is both a Chennai and a Bhubaneswar borrower. Geography suppressed
SEV 1 Agent identity is unrecoverable. 30,000 rows, 1,000 IDs, 1,099 employee codes, 10 distinct names. Every employee code maps to more than one person. No per-agent view
SEV 1 Call status contradicts call duration. 60% of calls report NO_ANSWER, BUSY or FAILED while carrying ~450 seconds of duration. No handle-time metrics
SEV 1 Channel attribution is structurally impossible. 36% of recovery has no interaction at all; 18% has two or more simultaneously. No channel ROI
SEV 2 Three timezones, no offsets. 67% of calls sit in the wrong hour bucket uncorrected; 8,924 fall on the wrong calendar day. Corrected
SEV 2 500 duplicate payments from ingestion replays and races. Note: deduplicating on payment_reference, the intuitive fix, would have deleted ₹60.8 Cr of real recovery. Corrected, −₹3.8 Cr
SEV 2 August 2026 is a partial month — data stops on the 8th. Any month-on-month including August shows a 74% collapse that did not happen. Excluded
